# Builder note: issue 118 (backup content validation), 11-10-2026

Refs #118. Branch fix-118 from origin/main b2a4cd6.

## What changed
- ops/backup/backup.sh: new `check_content` runs after `pg_restore -l` succeeds and before the dump is renamed to `.dump`. Failure: dump moved to `<name>.dump.rejected`, log line `FAILED content check: <reason>`, return 1 (no marker, no off-box copy, no rotation).
- Size rule: at least `BACKUP_MIN_SIZE_PERCENT` (50) of the newest previous `*.dump`; with none, at least `BACKUP_MIN_FIRST_BYTES` (1 MiB).
- TOC rule: `pg_restore -l` must contain `TABLE DATA public <t>` (optionally with a `_suffix`, so partitions count) for poll_log, pilot_session, network_snapshot, app_setting, pilot_position.
- `rotate` deletes oldest-first and keeps at least `BACKUP_MIN_KEEP` (3) `*.dump` files. `*.rejected` files are never counted and are removed after the retention period.
- `take_backup` ends with explicit `return 0` (once-mode exit status when the dump is good).
- Settings added to .env.example, docker-compose.yml, docs/CONFIG_MAP.md; paragraph "Dump acceptance" in docs/RUNBOOKS/restore.md.
- Tests: tests/test_backup_script.py (five new cases plus default fixture now has three old dumps).

## Thresholds
50 percent: nightly size moves by a few percent; halving means a wrong or emptied database, yet one bad day of retention pruning cannot trip it. 1 MiB: a schema-only dump is far smaller than a real one (not measured on production; check the real first dump size). Keep 3: survives two consecutive bad nights plus the weekly restore-test day.

## Watchdog
No watchdog change. A rejected dump is not a `*.dump`, so `backup_age` goes CRIT at 26 h, as for any failed backup. A dedicated "rejected" alert would need a watchdog change (not done, kept small).

## Not tested
No real pg_dump/pg_restore, no docker, no DB: fake tools only. Real TOC line format (`TABLE DATA public <name> <owner>`) is from knowledge of pg_restore -l, not checked against production output. Production partition names for pilot_position not checked (the pattern tolerates any suffix).

## Risks
- A legitimate halving of the database (big retention prune) is rejected until `BACKUP_MIN_SIZE_PERCENT` is lowered for a run; documented.
- If the previous dump was itself small-but-valid after an earlier bad state, baseline is that dump (newest `.dump`).
- The weak match `pilot_session` would also accept `pilot_session_*` tables; acceptable.
- Not addressed: restore.sh:76 silent exit, roles.sql rollback gap, 15:42 UTC partition note (not trivial or not code).
