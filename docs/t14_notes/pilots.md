## Needs owner input

1. **Card 135 (and 90) category names.** The current v1 ladder gives granular groups (for example "Boeing 737 Family", "Cessna 172", "Airbus A320 Family") and has known mis-classifications: the `'%C%'` test in the Business Jets branch catches every unmatched type containing the letter C (so a C172 is a "Business Jet" and never reaches "Cessna 172"), and A21N/A321neo falls into "Airbus A220 Family". The repo uses the coarser `aircraft_type` lookup (for example "Light piston", "Boeing 737 family"). Question: keep the lookup categories, or port v1's ladder as it is (bugs included), or port it with the C-test fixed?
2. **Card 61 breadth of exclusions.** v1's lists are short and named by hand. The lookup excludes about 230 more codes (many military variants, Citation and Falcon variants, further turboprops). Question: is the broader exclusion acceptable, or should the panel use only v1's explicit codes? The effect on a top-20 ranking is expected to be small. Also, v1's comment says business jets "E135 to E195 Embraer business jets"; confirm the E-series should still be excluded although they are airliners.
3. **Card 93 label for rating 11.** v1 names rating 11 "Administrator", but VATSIM's own scale is 11 = Supervisor and 12 = Administrator (see `controller_rating` in the seed). The panel copies v1. Question: keep v1's wording, or correct it (11 Supervisor, 12 Administrator)?
4. **Card 93 "enroute minutes".** The repo treats v1 `total_enroute_time_minutes` as airborne minutes. Question: does v1's enroute time mean airborne time, or the whole connected time between departure and arrival? It also decides the `> 0` filter of card 135.
5. **Card 55 total column.** v1 returns `total_movements`. Not added because the bar chart already shows both series. Say if the number should be visible.
